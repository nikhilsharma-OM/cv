\cvsubhead{Job Market Paper}
\pubentry{2026}{Manuscript ready for submission $\cdot$ M\&SOM}%
{Regulating Powerful Intermediaries: An Empirical Assessment in the Retail Pharmacy Supply Chain}%
{with John Gray and Jurriaan De Jong}%
{I examine regulatory actions taken by Ohio Medicaid targeting spread pricing, in which a Pharmacy Benefit Manager (PBM) bills the payer more for a drug than it reimburses the dispensing pharmacy. Using a difference-in-differences design on a coarsened exact-matched sample, complemented by an event-study analysis using synthetic controls, I find that payment transparency reduced Medicaid payer costs and lowered drug-quality risk, while a subsequent spread-pricing ban reversed a substantial portion of the cost savings. Presented at POMS 2025, DSI 2025, POMS 2026, and ISA 2026. First Place, Hayes Graduate Research Forum 2025.}

\cvsubhead{Under Review / Revise \& Resubmit}

{with Erin McKie, Arvind Chandrasekaran, and Sanghoon Cho}%
{A 48-week field experiment with 7,600 Midwest households testing AI-enabled curbside feedback and two types of green nudges on recycling contamination. Presented at the UIUC Supply Chain Management Conference 2025.}
